Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is Style BFF?

Style BFF is an AI personal stylist that learns your color season, body type, closet and plans, then shows outfit ideas on your own photo.

Is the Style Profile really free?

Yes. The Style Profile is free and does not require a card. It includes your 12-season color palette, body type and three personal style rules.

How is the outfit rendered on me?

You upload a clear photo. Style BFF uses it to create a visual try-on of the outfit while keeping the styling advice tied to your profile.

Can I add clothes I already own?

Yes. Style BFF can scan selected gallery photos to extract clothing you have worn and organize it in your digital wardrobe. You can also add pieces directly.

Can I try a Pinterest or Instagram outfit?

Yes. Share a supported link to the app and Style BFF turns the look into a try-on you can see on yourself.

What is a digital wardrobe?

It is a visual version of the clothes you own. Style BFF can scan selected gallery photos, extract the clothes you have worn, organize them into your wardrobe, build new combinations and help you create capsules.

What makes Style BFF different from an outfit generator?

Style BFF is built around you over time: your Style Profile, your digital wardrobe, your occasions and the looks you save. It can also show supported inspiration and product links on your own photo.

Does Style BFF work on Android?

Yes. Style BFF is available for iOS and Android.
